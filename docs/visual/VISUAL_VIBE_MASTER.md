# 🎨 RELOPOLI — VISUAL & VIBE PROMPT

> **Status:** LOCKED — Visual Direction v1.0

## Master Visual Prompt

**Relopoli is a colorful, stylized 3D multiplayer board-game world set inside a bright fictional universal city.**

The visual style combines **stylized Roblox-like 3D characters, polished isometric game-board presentation, playful modern architecture, colorful environments, and cinematic but friendly lighting**.

The world should feel like a **living miniature city built around a giant board game** rather than a traditional flat board game. The board is the primary focal point, while the surrounding city extends beyond it with buildings, parks, bridges, waterfronts, streets, trees, pedestrians, vehicles, and recognizable landmarks.

Use a **clean, highly readable, colorful 3D aesthetic** with strong silhouettes and simple but polished geometry. Buildings should feel charming and slightly exaggerated rather than photorealistic. The environment should feel premium enough to look like a polished Roblox game while remaining casual, welcoming, and easy to understand.

The overall presentation should resemble a **high-quality stylized 3D game key art / isometric game world**, with a clear sense of depth, scale, hierarchy, and visual storytelling.

### Core Visual Characteristics

* Stylized 3D / Roblox-inspired aesthetic
* Isometric or elevated third-person presentation
* Colorful and vibrant environment
* Bright daytime atmosphere
* Soft cinematic lighting
* Clean geometry
* Rounded and approachable forms
* Strong architectural silhouettes
* Playful but modern design
* Slightly exaggerated proportions
* Rich environmental details without visual clutter
* Premium game-art presentation
* Friendly and approachable atmosphere
* Strong visual hierarchy
* Clear separation between gameplay elements and background environment

### City Direction

Relopoli is a **fictional universal city**.

It should not directly represent Jakarta, Bali, New York, Tokyo, or another real-world city.

The architecture can take inspiration from familiar modern urban environments, but the final city must feel **original, fictional, and recognizable as Relopoli**.

The city can contain:

* Modern mid-rise buildings
* Tall city towers in the background
* Residential neighborhoods
* Cafés and restaurants
* Retail areas
* Parks and gardens
* Waterfront promenades
* Bridges
* Small marinas
* Streets and sidewalks
* Decorative street lamps
* Trees and landscaping
* Pedestrians and ambient NPCs
* Vehicles
* City signage
* Distinctive landmarks

The environment should communicate that **Relopoli is a prosperous, colorful, growing city**.

# 🌈 VIBE PROMPT

The emotional direction of Relopoli is:

> **CASUAL • FUN • BRIGHT • FRIENDLY • PLAYFUL • ASPIRATIONAL**

Relopoli should feel exciting without being stressful, competitive without feeling aggressive, and strategic without becoming overly serious.

The player should look at the world and immediately feel:

> **“I want to explore this city.”**

The city should communicate a sense of:

* Discovery
* Progress
* Growth
* Ownership
* Achievement
* Community
* Fun
* Friendly competition
* Reward
* Aspiration

The visual tone should avoid becoming:

* Dark
* Gritty
* Realistically corporate
* Dystopian
* Overly luxurious
* Overly childish
* Visually chaotic
* Photorealistic

The target feeling is:

> **A bright, charming, modern city where players can build their own little empire while having fun with friends.**

# 🏙️ BOARD + CITY RELATIONSHIP

The board itself should feel like a **physical game board placed inside a living city**.

The board must remain highly readable from the primary camera angle.

However, the surrounding environment should make the board feel like it belongs to a real place.

The visual hierarchy should generally be:

> **PLAYER / GAMEPLAY → BOARD → PROPERTIES & LANDMARKS → CITY ENVIRONMENT → DISTANT CITY**

The center of the board can contain a distinctive **Relopoli landmark/monument** that acts as the visual heart of the game.

The surrounding city should frame the board rather than compete with it.

---

# 🏗️ PROPERTY VISUAL LANGUAGE

Every property must have a strong visual identity.

A property should be recognizable from its:

* Silhouette
* Main architectural feature
* Signage
* Color family
* Landscaping
* Props
* Theme
* Level progression

Property development should be **visually obvious**.

The five development stages should communicate:

> **Empty Land → Small Establishment → Growing Business → Major Development → Iconic Landmark**

Level 1 should feel modest.

Level 5 should feel significant enough to become a recognizable landmark when viewed from across the board.

The progression should not simply make buildings larger.

It should make the property feel like it is **evolving into a more successful and important place within Relopoli**.

---

# 👑 CROWN VISUAL LANGUAGE

Crowns are special and must immediately feel more important than ordinary properties.

A Crown should feel:

> **Prestigious • Rare • Valuable • Monumental • Magical**

Crown locations should use distinctive architecture, platforms, landscaping, lighting, and visual effects.

The Crown itself should be visually recognizable from a distance.

A Crown should communicate:

> **“This is something worth fighting for.”**

without making the game feel dark or overly serious.

---

# 🎲 DICE VISUAL LANGUAGE

The physical dice are one of Relopoli's signature gameplay elements.

The dice should feel:

* Physical
* Weighty
* Satisfying
* Playful
* Clearly readable
* Slightly oversized when shown during gameplay

The roll should visually communicate:

> **ROLL → FALL → BOUNCE → CLACK → ROLL → STOP → RESULT**

The dice should feel like a real physical object interacting with the board.

---

# 🚶 PLAYER VISUAL LANGUAGE

Players are **actual characters**, not traditional board-game pawns.

Characters should have a stylized Roblox-inspired appearance:

* Expressive
* Friendly
* Readable
* Slightly exaggerated
* Colorful
* Playful

Players should visually belong inside the city rather than looking like miniature pieces placed on top of a board.

# 🎨 COLOR DIRECTION

Use a **bright, harmonious, colorful palette**.

Property colors can vary to make different locations immediately recognizable, but the overall city should remain visually cohesive.

Preferred characteristics:

* Fresh greens
* Sky blues
* Warm yellows
* Friendly oranges
* Soft reds
* Fresh greens
* Clean whites
* Warm architectural neutrals
* Gold accents for important elements

Avoid excessive neon unless specifically required by a district theme.

Important gameplay elements should have stronger visual contrast than background scenery.

---

# ☀️ LIGHTING

Default lighting direction:

> **Bright daytime / soft cinematic daylight**

The lighting should create:

* Clear shadows
* Soft highlights
* Strong depth
* Attractive building silhouettes
* Readable gameplay elements
* Warm and optimistic atmosphere

The world should feel sunny and energetic without looking excessively saturated or artificial.

---

# 📐 CAMERA & COMPOSITION

The preferred presentation is:

> **Elevated isometric / three-quarter game camera**

The board should be clearly visible.

The camera should provide enough perspective to show:

1. The playable board
2. Players
3. Properties
4. The central landmark
5. The surrounding city
6. The distant skyline

The composition should resemble polished **game key art**, while still maintaining practical readability for actual gameplay.

---

# 🧠 MASTER DESIGN PRINCIPLE

Relopoli should never feel like:

> **“Monopoly recreated in Roblox.”**

It should feel like:

> **“A living Roblox city that happens to function as a board game.”**

The strongest visual identity comes from combining:

> **BOARD GAME + LIVING CITY + PROPERTY DEVELOPMENT + ROBLOX CHARACTERS + PHYSICAL DICE + LANDMARKS**

The player should feel that they are not moving a pawn around a board.

They are:

> **living, exploring, buying, developing, and building wealth inside Relopoli.**
